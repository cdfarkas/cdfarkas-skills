# Nimbus AI — Customer Data Processing Addendum

This Data Processing Addendum forms part of the Nimbus AI Terms of Service between Nimbus AI
Inc. ("Nimbus") and the customer ("Customer").

## 1. Scope and roles

1.1 Customer is the controller and Nimbus the processor of Customer Personal Data submitted
to the Nimbus API. The subject-matter of the processing is the provision of the API services;
the duration is the term of the Terms of Service.
1.2 Nature and purpose: Nimbus receives prompts and files, generates completions, and stores
inputs and outputs to provide the services.
1.3 Personal data: any personal data Customer chooses to include in inputs. Data subjects:
any individual whose personal data Customer includes.

## 2. Processing

2.1 Nimbus processes Customer Personal Data on Customer's documented instructions, which are
the Terms of Service, this Addendum and Customer's use of the API settings.
2.2 Notwithstanding 2.1, Nimbus may use Customer Personal Data to improve and train its models
and services, unless Customer opts out through the account settings. Nimbus may also create
aggregated or de-identified data and use it for its own purposes.

## 3. Personnel and security

3.1 Nimbus ensures its personnel are subject to confidentiality obligations.
3.2 Nimbus maintains the technical and organisational security measures described at
nimbus.example/security, which it may update from time to time.

## 4. Sub-processors

4.1 Customer grants a general authorisation for Nimbus to engage sub-processors listed at
nimbus.example/subprocessors. Nimbus will update that page before adding a new
sub-processor. Nimbus imposes data protection terms on sub-processors that are no less
protective than this Addendum and remains liable for their performance.

## 5. Assistance

5.1 Taking into account the nature of the processing, Nimbus will provide reasonable
assistance with data subject requests and with Customer's obligations under Articles 32 to 36
GDPR, including data protection impact assessments.

## 6. Security incidents

6.1 Nimbus will notify Customer of a personal data breach without undue delay.

## 7. International transfers

7.1 Nimbus processes data in the United States. Transfers from the EEA rely on the Standard
Contractual Clauses (Module Two), incorporated by reference.

## 8. Deletion

8.1 Upon termination, Nimbus deletes Customer Personal Data within 90 days, except for data
retained in backups, which is kept according to Nimbus's retention schedule.

## 9. Audits

9.1 Nimbus makes available its SOC 2 Type II report on request. Customer may audit Nimbus
only where required by a supervisory authority, at Customer's cost.

## 10. Liability

10.1 Each party's liability under this Addendum is subject to the limitations of liability in
the Terms of Service.
