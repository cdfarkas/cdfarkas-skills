# Legal decisions

Only answers validated in writing by a professional (lawyer, DPO, the CNIL) go here, with
the date and the source document. Official-source readings and unvalidated opinions do not.
Open questions go to `docs/lawyer-questions.md`; once answered and validated, they are logged here.
If the current situation differs from an entry's Conditions, the entry does not apply.

## Entry template

```
### <short title>
- Question:
- Answer:
- Conditions: (facts the answer depends on; if any changes, the entry no longer applies)
- Validated by: (role, not name, e.g. "DPO", "lawyer specialised in health data")
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Source document: (path or reference of the written answer)
```

### Audio retention after transcription
- Question: How long may session audio recordings be kept once they are transcribed?
- Answer: Delete the audio within 30 days of transcription; keep only the transcript.
- Conditions: transcription runs locally on the therapist's laptop; the audio never leaves
  that laptop; full-disk encryption is on; no third-party provider is involved.
- Validated by: DPO
- Date: 2026-03-12
- Source document: DPO's written answer, email of 2026-03-12, archived as docs/legal/dpo-answer-2026-03-12.pdf
